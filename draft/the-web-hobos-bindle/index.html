The Web has lost its innocence.

It didn’t happen overnight of course, it was a gradual thing. But we seem to have realized it fairly abruptly, fairly recently. The realities of the Faustian bargains we have been making over the last ten, twenty years have come into the foreground. As consumers, we have traded privacy for free services. As content creators, we have traded convenience and free hosting for ownership. But as the downsides of those bargains become more visible, due to a combination of legislation forcing the costs into the open () and a certain arrogance Exploitation has become more Blatant and shameless.
